About

Engineering, research, reading, and writing

I like difficult problems that lead to something useful.

I've worked in data science, deep learning, infrastructure, and software engineering. I like problems where I need to understand the details and then make something work.

Current location

  • Based in Europe.
  • Interested in autonomous systems, efficient ML, and research engineering.
  • I spend a lot of my time outside work reading, writing, and improving my tools.

Working style

How I tend to approach problems

  • I want to know what a system is doing. An abstraction is useful if it still makes sense when something unusual happens.
  • I care about speed. Often the biggest improvement comes from a better interface, fewer handoffs, or finding out sooner whether a change worked.
  • I'll usually read the source code and trace what happens to the data before changing anything. It takes time, but I'd rather understand why something breaks.
  • I'm comfortable working on an algorithm and on the infrastructure needed to run it.

Outside work

Outside work, I read, write essays, and work on side projects. A lot of them come back to economics, machine learning, coordination problems, or the history of technology.

I've kept this site fairly simple. Some of the research figures are interactive because seeing a process move can make it easier to understand.
