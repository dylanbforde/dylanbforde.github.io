Writing

Essays on incentives, technology, economics, and systems

Notes from the overlap between machine learning and social theory.

Most of these essays try to take one technical frame, optimization, compounding, coordination, search, and use it to think more clearly about human systems.

Why write?

Writing is where I pressure-test intuitions that are too fuzzy to deserve confidence yet. The essays are usually trying to explain a mechanism, not just take a stance.
