Computational biology / survival modeling

LUAD survival modeling.

This research workflow combines TCGA-LUAD expression and clinical data, uses survival models and regulatory signals, and brings treatment rules into explainable reports.

Explore the workflowView source ↗

Central challenge

Gene expression is high-dimensional, survival outcomes are censored, and clinical records are incomplete. Before molecular and clinical signals can be combined, each step that reduces this complexity needs to be explicit.

TCGA-LUADCox modelsR

Interactive workflow

Bring the evidence together in the report.

The workflow keeps expression-derived risk separate from clinical biomarker rules until it generates the combined report.

Input Core Output

Case study

Working with incomplete biological data

01 / Problem

Data with different scales and gaps

Expression matrices, survival endpoints, biomarkers, and clinical variables have different scales and different patterns of missing data.

02 / System

Model risk separately from clinical rules

Univariate Cox screening and Lasso-Cox produce a risk signature. Clinical factors go into a separate R6 recommendation layer.

03 / Artifact

Analysis and report generation

The repository contains the TCGA workflow, survival analysis, treatment rules, regulatory-network context, and report generation.

Workflow detail

From cohort to explainable output

  1. Prepare the cohort

    Filter the tumor samples, then align molecular measurements with usable clinical outcomes.

  2. Select survival signal

    Screen with Cox models, then use penalized Cox modeling to construct a risk score.

  3. Apply clinical rules

    Use a separate recommendation system to interpret biomarkers and patient factors.

  4. Compose the report

    Present the risk, clinical context, and reasons for each recommendation while keeping their provenance clear.

Evidence boundary

Research and education.

This work is for research and education, not clinical decision support. The cohort summaries and model outputs reported in the repository describe its analysis. They do not establish clinical validity or authorize treatment decisions.
