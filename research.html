For the reported two-step refinement path, the four staircase plan factors can be reconstructed from one resident reference tile and explicit row and column modifiers.

02 · Hardware-aware schedule

Stream the active support

Fixed-width block-wise execution yields O((T+R)LW) work, O(Ld) input storage, and O(L) additional HBM for fixed head dimension and band width.

03 · Structured gaps

Dustbin augmentation bridge

The implemented gap-aware path is formalized as the same balanced surrogate on an augmented support, allowing the adjoint schedule to lift without claiming a general unbalanced model.

Evidence boundary

The optimized masked kernel matches exact autodiff of the same centered surrogate on synthetic checks. TPU experiments establish trainability and systems stability for the promoted balanced path; the paper reports mixed downstream alignment results and keeps those claims deliberately separate from kernel exactness.

01 · Derivative structure

A walk behind the gradient

Each normalization cycle induces a stochastic operator on column-potential perturbations modulo constants. Its transpose carries zero-mass reverse-mode signals.

02 · Uniform guarantees

Every feasible face matters

At balanced plans, score-uniform one-step contraction requires pairwise two-hop column overlap on every feasible face of the transportation polytope.

03 · Sparse schedules

Mixing across several steps

Ordered support schedules can provide contraction over a window even when an individual layer has no global one-step guarantee.

Scope of the result

The guarantees concern the fixed-support quotient-gradient component. The illustration uses balanced toy plans and omits score and marginal source terms. Finite Sinkhorn cycles generally use two distinct half-step plans; the single-plan walk shown here is the balanced case. A coefficient of one withholds a one-step guarantee; it does not rule out later mixing.
